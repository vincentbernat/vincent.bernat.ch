--- title: "A non-interactive tour of the spanning tree protocol" description: | Root election, port roles, proposals and agreements, topology changes: watch RSTP in action through simulations powered by MSTPD. uuid: 34411fc3-24cd-4d4f-bd24-a8822bfc9b98 cover: stp-dragonblood@2x.jpg created: !!timestamp 2026-08-24 14:59:00 tags: - network --- Imagine you rent office space for a three-day event. You quickly set up a few Ethernet switches and tape some cables on the floor to get everyone online. Unfortunately, Stan, your clumsiest coworker, kicks out a cable every time he gets up for coffee. Spare cables would fix that, but a loop turns into a broadcast storm: Ethernet packets multiply until nothing else gets through. That’s where the *spanning tree protocol* comes in: it blocks just enough of the spare cables to leave a loop-free tree, and rebuilds it in a second each time Stan strikes again.[^credits] [^credits]: The sprites for Stan and Blobby come from [Craftpix][].
